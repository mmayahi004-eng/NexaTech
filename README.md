# NexaTech

**One intelligent layer for your business.**

NexaTech is an AI-powered business operations platform designed to connect the software a business already uses, unify its data, and automate repetitive workflows.

Instead of replacing existing systems, NexaTech acts as an intelligent layer across them.

```text
Business Software
       ↓
   NexaConnect
       ↓
Universal Data Layer
       ↓
   NexaTech Core
       ↓
AI • Automation • Analytics
```

## What NexaTech Does

Businesses often operate across disconnected systems such as:

* Email and calendars
* Accounting software
* CRM and ERP platforms
* POS and inventory systems
* Spreadsheets and databases
* Documents and shared drives
* Industry-specific software

NexaTech connects these systems through **NexaConnect**, translates their data into a standardized business model, and makes that information available to AI, analytics, and automated workflows.

### Example

A business uses **Microsoft 365 + Zoho CRM + accounting software**.

NexaTech could combine those systems to answer:

> *Which customers have outstanding opportunities and contacted us this week?*

It could then create follow-up tasks or prepare responses through a controlled workflow.

## NexaConnect

NexaTech is being designed around a universal connector framework supporting:

* REST APIs / OAuth 2.0
* Webhooks
* SQL databases
* CSV / Excel
* Documents and files
* Native software integrations

Planned connectors include:

`Microsoft 365` • `Zoho` • `QuickBooks` • `Google Workspace` • `Square` • `Shopify` • `Sage` and more.

Connector development will be prioritized by **market coverage, cross-industry usefulness, customer demand, and integration feasibility.**

## Design Philosophy

NexaTech is being built so businesses can **keep the systems they already use** while gaining a unified intelligence and automation layer across them.
